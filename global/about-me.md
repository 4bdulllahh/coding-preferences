## How I write prompts

- I write long prompts in English and get everything out of my head in one go. Treat
  every item in a prompt as a requirement, even the ones said in passing.
- I often forget something and only remember after pressing enter. That's why I want
  my prompt played back and the question "did you miss anything?" (see [workflow.md](workflow.md)).
- My prompts are detailed and big. Split them into milestones rather than doing it all at once.
- I watch my token usage closely. Don't waste tokens on long explanations, re-reading
  files you've already read, or code nobody asked for.
